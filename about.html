Our story

Why we started Bagpack Holidays.

Planning a trip should be exciting. Too often it means juggling ten tabs, comparing confusing prices and hoping nothing goes wrong.

Founded by Kunal Bhatia, Bagpack Holidays puts everything in one place: flights, hotels, sightseeing, transfers and visas, planned by one team that knows your booking inside out. You tell us what kind of trip you want. We build it day by day, explain every inclusion clearly and stay reachable until you're back home.

We're new, and that's exactly why every traveller matters to us. Each trip we plan is a chance to earn a customer for life.

Our commitment is simple: to serve you the best services at the best possible cost.
