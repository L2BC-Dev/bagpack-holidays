FAQ

Good to know.

How quickly will I get a quote?

During working hours (10 AM to 8 PM, all 7 days) we usually reply on WhatsApp within minutes. A full custom itinerary is typically ready within a few hours, depending on the destination.

Can you customise a package?

Yes. Every trip is built around you. Change the hotels, add days, swap activities or plan around a birthday or anniversary.

Do you only do full packages, or can I book just a flight or hotel?

Both. Book a single flight ticket, just a hotel, only visa help or a complete holiday, whatever you need.

What's usually not included in a package?

Typically travel insurance, visa fees (if applicable), early check-in or late check-out charges, tips, personal expenses and meals not mentioned in the itinerary. Every quote lists the inclusions and exclusions clearly.

Can I visit your office?

Of course. We're at 267, Sector 17, Faridabad, Haryana 121001. A quick call before you visit helps us make sure the right person is available.
