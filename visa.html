General checklist

Documents to keep ready.

Most tourist visas ask for some mix of these. Having them ready speeds everything up.

Valid passport

At least 6 months validity from your return date, with 2+ blank pages.

Photographs

Recent passport-size photos. Size and background differ by country.

Bank statements

Usually the last 3 to 6 months, stamped by the bank, showing enough funds for the trip.

ITR / income proof

Income tax returns, salary slips or business documents for most sticker visas.

Flight bookings

Confirmed or dummy return tickets, depending on the visa rules.

Hotel bookings

Stay confirmation for every night of the trip.

Travel insurance

Mandatory for Schengen and recommended everywhere else.

Cover letter

Explains your trip purpose and plans. We draft it for you.
